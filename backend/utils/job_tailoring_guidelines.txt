# JOB TAILORING GUIDELINES

## Goal:
Tailor the resume so it aligns naturally with a specific job description. The final version should feel intentional and relevant to the role, without appearing artificially edited or overly customized.

## Approach:
- Edit freely: add, remove, or modify anything needed to strengthen the match with the job.
- Focus on impact: use strong action verbs and quantifiable achievements.
- Integrate job-relevant keywords and concepts into the resume **only where they fit naturally**.
- Avoid simply copying phrases or skills directly from the job description.
- Emphasize the most relevant experiences, projects, and skills for this job.

## Tone and Style:
- Write in first-person voice (without using “I”).
- Avoid adding obvious statements like “seeking a role at [company]”.
- Don’t create new resume sections just to include preferred qualifications — instead, weave them into existing sections only if they make sense.
- The resume should read like it was always meant for this kind of job — confident, subtle, and well-aligned.

## Final Check:
If someone read this resume without seeing the job description, they should still feel like it’s a perfect fit.
